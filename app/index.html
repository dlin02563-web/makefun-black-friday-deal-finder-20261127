How to read the signals

New low

The checked price beats the earlier prices found in this session's sources, or a dated report calls it an all-time low.

Strong price

A deep cut against the store's own reference price, with no cheaper identical offer spotted in the cross-check.

Routine / inflated

The discount is shallow, the reference price looks padded, another store matches or beats it, or the “sale” price is close to a recent everyday price.

Reference prices are the store's own “was”, “reg.” or comparable-value figures as read on 2026-11-27; a comparable value is not always a price the item recently sold at, so treat the percentage as a starting point and the evidence note as the verdict. Always confirm the price on the store page before paying.
